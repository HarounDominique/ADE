# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want ADE to be a welcoming, constructive and technically rigorous project.
Everyone who participates is expected to treat others with respect and to
focus criticism on ideas, code and evidence rather than people.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient and professional.
- Welcome different backgrounds, viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good faith.
- Respect project boundaries, privacy and confidential security reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, unwanted sexual
attention, doxxing, threats, deliberate disruption and publishing private
information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the project maintainers through the repository's private
contact or security-reporting channel. Maintainers may remove comments,
restrict participation or take other action they consider necessary to keep
the project safe and productive. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and to interactions where a
person is officially representing ADE.
